The studio

Two Sparks is my independent software studio. It’s where I design, build, and launch my own products and help others bring theirs to life. Every app that carries the name gets the same treatment: a clear idea, careful design, and solid engineering underneath.

I work across the whole stack of a product, from shaping the concept and designing the interface to writing the code, preparing the launch, and looking after it once it’s out in the world.

Great software starts with a small spark of an idea, and becomes something special when craft and care meet.

What I’ve been building

Viisi is a daily word puzzle for iPhone that respects its players, with no ads, no accounts, and no data collection. CastLink is a web app that untangles the connections between actors, movies, and TV shows. Different products for different audiences, built with the same approach: focus on the experience, then sweat the details.

What I care about

Craft over clutter

Fewer, better features. Every screen earns its place.

Respect for users

Privacy first, no dark patterns, and accessible by default.

Shipping matters

Ideas are great, but real products in real hands are better.

Clear communication

Honest timelines, regular updates, and no jargon.
