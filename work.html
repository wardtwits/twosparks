Viisi

Five letters. Five words. One puzzle.

Viisi is a daily anagram word game for iPhone. Every day the whole community gets the same five letters, and the challenge is to find five valid words using them. It’s built as a calm, thoughtful alternative for players who want a little more depth from their daily puzzle.

  • Daily shared puzzle with a new set of letters every morning
  • Hints that unlock gently, plus an Expert Mode with no hints at all
  • Stats, streaks & badges along with a full archive of past puzzles
  • No ads, no account, works offline and privacy first by design
  • iOS
  • Game design
  • UI/UX
  • Marketing site
Visit viisi.app

CastLink

Find the connections between the screen’s biggest names.

CastLink is a web app for movie and TV fans who have ever asked “where have I seen them before?” Compare any two titles to see the cast they share, compare two actors to see the titles they’ve both appeared in, or trace the path from any film actor back to Kevin Bacon.

  • Shared cast between any movies or TV shows
  • Shared titles between any two actors
  • Degrees of Kevin Bacon with movie-only connection paths
  • Powered by TMDB for up-to-date film and TV data
  • Web app
  • API integration
  • Graph search
  • UI/UX
Visit castlink.app
