How I build

A few rules every app follows.

No ads

Nothing between you and the thing you opened the app to do.

No accounts

Open it and use it. There’s nothing to sign up for.

No tracking

No analytics SDKs and no ad trackers, in any of them.

Offline where possible

Viisi plays without a connection, and CSS Scratchpad keeps everything in your browser. CastLink needs the network to look things up.

Accessible

I aim for readable contrast, keyboard and screen reader support, and respect for reduced-motion settings.

Small and focused

Each app does one job. If a feature doesn’t help with that job, it stays out.

Found a bug? Have an idea?

If something in one of my apps isn’t working, or you wish it did one more thing, I’d like to hear about it. Hello is welcome too.
